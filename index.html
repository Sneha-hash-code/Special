Chapter 01

The Beginning

Somewhere around Class 4 or 5, two kids sat in the same school without ever once finding each other.

Years later, both of us ended up in Birgunj — still strangers, still walking past a story that hadn’t started yet.

It took until Class 11, the same college, for our paths to actually cross. As if the story had been waiting for the right chapter to begin. No dramatic beginning. No warning. No idea that something ordinary was about to become something unforgettable. Looking back now, I think that was one of those tiny moments life quietly changes everything.

Some beginnings take the long way. This one was worth the wait.

Chapter 08

One Last Thing…

There was one more thing I wanted you to see…

tap to open

My Angrez,

I don't really know how to begin this letter, because every beginning feels too small for everything I want to say.

Maybe I should begin with the fact that we were once just two people in the same school, walking past each other without knowing that one day our lives would somehow find their way toward each other. Or maybe I should begin with Class 11. The year when you stopped being just another person in the world and slowly became someone I couldn't imagine my days without.

I think about our walks sometimes. Kathmandu. Lalitpur. The random conversations. The silly moments. The days that didn't seem extraordinary while we were living them.Funny how life works. We never knew we were making memories while we were making them.

And now you're far away.

Sometimes I wish I could shrink the distance into something I could hold in my hand and simply throw away.

I wish time zones didn't exist. I wish there wasn't an ocean between “I miss you” and “I'm here.”

But even with all those miles, somehow you still find your way into the smallest parts of my day.

Something happens and I think, “I need to tell you this. I LOVE YOU”

I see something funny and wonder what your reaction would be.

I remember something from years ago and suddenly I'm smiling at my screen like an idiot.

And I think that's when I realized how deeply you've become a part of my life.

Not loudly. Not dramatically. Just quietly... in all the little places.

I want you to know that I'm proud of you. Proud of the person you're becoming. Proud of the dreams you're chasing. Proud of every difficult step you take even when nobody sees it.

And on your birthday, more than anything, I hope life is kind to you.

I hope you get the future you've worked so hard for.

I hope you laugh until your stomach hurts. I hope you find peace in places you never expected. I hope you meet people who remind you how wonderful you are.

And I hope that somewhere along the way, life gives us another ordinary day together.

No ocean. No time-zone math. No “I wish you were here.”

Just you. Just me. And another completely ordinary day that somehow becomes one of my favorite memories.

If I could give you one thing today, it wouldn't be a gift.

It would be the ability to see yourself through the eyes of the people who love you.

Maybe then you'd understand just how special you are.

Thank you for becoming one of the most beautiful chapters of my life.

And if this story has taught me anything, it's that some people enter your life so naturally that you don't realize how much they've changed it until you imagine it without them.

I don't want to imagine mine without you.

And maybe one day, when we're older, we'll look back at all of this and laugh about how far away we once had to be.

Because one day, I don't want there to be any distance left between us.

Happy Birthday, my Angrez. ♡

Until then...
keep chasing your dreams.
I'll be cheering from here.

Always,
Sneha
